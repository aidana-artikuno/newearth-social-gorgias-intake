1. How social media connects to Gorgias

Gorgias does not “watch the internet.” It connects to a Facebook Business Page. Instagram is added only through that same page. Replies sent from Gorgias appear on Facebook or Instagram as if someone typed them in the native app.

What we need from New Earth to connect

  • A Facebook Business Page (not a personal profile, not a Facebook Group).
  • A professional Instagram account, in the same Meta Business portfolio, linked to that Page.
  • A New Earth admin who can click Login with Facebook in Gorgias and accept all requested permissions. Artikuno cannot complete this without that login.

Once connected, we choose per page which activity creates tickets: Messenger, Instagram DMs, comments, ad comments, mentions, and (Facebook only) page recommendations. We can turn Instagram on and leave Facebook comments off, or the reverse.

Not native in Gorgias social inbox: TikTok organic comments, YouTube, X / Twitter, LinkedIn. TikTok Shop is a separate order-support app, not a replacement for Meta comments and DMs. WhatsApp is a different channel if you want it later.

2. What shows up in Gorgias

Each inbound interaction we enable becomes a ticket in newearth-prod, next to email and chat. Eliza would work it from the same helpdesk: customer history, Shopify order sidebar, macros, tags, and rules.

Instagram DM / Facebook Messenger Yes, if enabled Reply privately. Same Shopify context as email. Auto-responder available on Messenger.
Comments on posts, Reels, ads Yes — every top-level comment is its own ticket Public reply, private reply (take it to DM), hide/unhide. Facebook: Like / Unlike only.
Mentions (@New Earth) Yes, if enabled Like / public reply. Cannot hide a Facebook mention or reply to it privately via Messenger.
Facebook recommendations / reviews Yes, if enabled Reply in-thread like a comment ticket.
Likes, reactions, story views, follows No These do not create tickets. Gorgias cannot send Love / Care / Haha / etc.
Replies to replies (nested comments) Usually no new ticket We can only reply to the top-level comment from Gorgias, not to a reply-to-a-reply.
Every comment we pull in is a Gorgias ticket. A busy ad or affiliate post can create dozens of tickets in an hour. That is why we will not turn every box on until you tell us volume and what Customer Care should own versus marketing.

3. How it would be handled (draft SOP for Eliza)

This is the operating model we would implement after you complete the questionnaire. We will adjust it to your answers.

Inbox and ownership

  • Social tickets land in a dedicated Social view (Instagram + Facebook), assigned to Eliza during New Earth hours (Mon–Fri, 8:00 AM–5:00 PM Pacific), unless you name a different owner.
  • Marketing / community can keep posting natively. Support replies go out from Gorgias so we have a record and can use Shopify + macros.
  • Same customer who emails and DMs is one Gorgias customer profile — no more split conversations.

Direct messages (private)

  1. Treat like email: identify the customer, check Shopify, use existing macros (login, subscription, shipping, promo policy).
  2. Do not invent discount codes. Live offers only from the current Help Center / promo macros.
  3. Cancel / stop-charge language still hits the existing high-priority escalate-to-Eliza rule.
  4. Medical / “will this treat X?” → existing medical-acknowledgment path; no product claims.
  5. Affiliate / commission questions → existing commission macros or Kevin / Ann as you specify below.

Public comments

  1. Simple praise (“love this”) — short public thank-you, or hide-from-queue if you tell us praise does not need a human. Liking a Facebook comment without a reply is not a billable interaction.
  2. Support question on a post — short public acknowledgment, then Reply by DM to finish (order lookups and account changes do not belong in a public thread).
  3. Spam, scams, off-topic, abusive — hide from Gorgias (hidden on the platform for everyone except the author). Optional rule: auto-hide on keywords you list (crypto, “DM me for a code,” competitor bait, etc.).
  4. Angry or public complaint — do not argue in comments. Hide if it is abusive; otherwise acknowledge once and move to DM. Escalate internally if it is legal, medical harm, or press.
  5. Promo / “what’s the code?” — use the current promo macro; never invent a code. If no live offer, say so and point to newsletter / SMS signup as you do on email.

After hours and Meta’s reply windows

  • Outside 8–5 PT weekdays, social waits like email unless you want a Messenger auto-responder (“we’ll reply next business day”).
  • Humans can reply to a Messenger / Instagram DM in Gorgias only if the customer’s last message was within 7 days. Miss that window: reply natively on Meta, or wait for the customer to write again.

4. What we can filter, hide, or leave out

We can do this

  • Connect only the channels you want (e.g. Instagram DMs only).
  • Leave ad comments or mentions off if volume is noise.
  • Rules that auto-hide comments matching keywords.
  • Rules that auto-tag and close “thanks / love this” with no reply.
  • Hide a comment from the ticket (Eliza or a rule).
  • Take a public comment private via DM.

We cannot do this in Gorgias

  • Ingest “all reactions” as tickets (and we should not).
  • Reply to nested comment threads (reply-to-a-reply).
  • See that Facebook already auto-hid a comment — it still appears as a normal ticket.
  • Connect Facebook Groups.
  • Moderate TikTok / YouTube / X from the same native inbox.

5. Why we are asking these questions first

Connecting social without a scope usually means: the inbox fills with ad-comment noise, Eliza is pulled off email, and nobody agrees who owns a public fight on a post. Your answers let us decide whether to connect, which boxes to tick, and how to staff Eliza before go-live.
